Flat content card (18px radius, surface fill, no shadow by default) for topics, regions and articles.

```jsx
<Card kicker="Função executiva" title="Controle inibitório" body="Frear respostas automáticas." meta={<><i className="ph ph-clock" />6 min</>} interactive />
```

- `elevation` sm|md|lg only for floating things. Never add a coloured left border.
