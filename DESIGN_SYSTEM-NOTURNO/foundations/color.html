The ramps are the same in both themes; the roles (--color-bg, --color-text, --color-link…) are what flip. Dark is the default; add data-theme="light" to <html> — or to a single <section> to flip just that band. Blue as a fill is --color-accent (#0071e3); blue as text is --color-link (#2997ff on dark, #0066cc on light), tuned for contrast.
